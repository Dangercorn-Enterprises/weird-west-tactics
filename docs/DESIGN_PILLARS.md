# DUSTFALL — Design Pillars (PROPOSED, v0.1 — 2026-07-09)

*Derived from Tim's design canon (StarCraft/TW:WH3, RimWorld/Nemesis, New Vegas,
FF6, BattleTech fire-support doctrine). Tim is game director — these are
proposed until he blesses/edits them. Every GUTS-pass fix gets framed against
these pillars; a fix that serves no pillar doesn't ship.*

## P1 — Every archetype is a different game
(StarCraft's three races; TW:WH3's "each lord is a different game on one engine")
Six archetypes and six gods should each warp the rules, not reskin them.
Asymmetry made fair — distinct doctrines, balanced by feel + the sim harness.
**Current sin:** 5/9 dead stats + no LOS flatten everyone into "ranged dude
with a different hat."

## P2 — Position wins wars (the Archer doctrine)
Fire support, arcs, sight lines, range control. The player who thinks two turns
ahead should beat the player with bigger numbers. LOS, indirect fire, spotting,
directional cover — the mechanics that make a missile-boat brain smile.
**Current sin:** no line-of-sight at all; bullets pass through mesas; cover is
omnidirectional; positioning has two axes when it needs five.

## P3 — The frontier remembers
(RimWorld's storyteller, the Nemesis callout, New Vegas factions)
Systems that generate stories beat authored content per dollar. Named rivals
who survive, scar, escalate, and hold grudges; a world that reacts; wounds
that mean something. Replayability through generated CHARACTERS, not maps.
**Current sin:** every fight is disposable; nothing remembers you; unloseable.

## P4 — Six gods, six rulebooks
Favor is the signature economy — deepen it until god choice is build identity
(and the shrine swear actually works — known port regression). Gods with fine
print, faction-flavored reactions, prices that aren't posted.
**Current sin:** god choice is near-cosmetic; divine specs in design.json
unimplemented.

## P5 — Scope like FF6
"Near perfect for story and scope." Depth over length: a shaped, replayable
8-15 hours beats a padded 40. Real loss states and ending variation, then
stop. Kill features that don't serve a pillar.

directs → prototype → playtest together → tune. No autonomous design.*
